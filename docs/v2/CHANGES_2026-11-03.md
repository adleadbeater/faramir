# Changes of 2026-11-03 (Rob's decisions after Omar's feedback)

1. **Chart timing.** One waiting job polls The Numbers every 5 minutes from 10am ET instead of relying on GitHub's
   dropped 15-minute cron (`chart_watch.wait`, `daily.yml` job `wait`). Charts with no reporting history are ready once
   unchanged for 45 minutes. Old sparse-check timings are never learned from.
2. **Inflation-adjusted comparisons.** Films released 25+ years before the data date are restated in 2024 dollars with
   the US average ticket price (`data/ticket_prices.json`, sourced per year; `faramir/inflation.py`). Films before 1955
   have no verified price and are no longer comparison films. Cards show the raw and the restated dollars, the two ticket
   prices and the method; worldwide is flagged approximate. Titles may not quote a classic's dollars.
3. **Modern vs. Classic** is its own card label: a Chase or Overtake against a classic-era film, compared in today's
   dollars. A classic in the film's own main genre counts as a real link (`classic_same_genre`).
4. **Daily floor of two cards** (`config.DAILY_CARD_FLOOR`, priority config v3 `min_cards`). Weak stories (strength < 0.4)
   are promoted, flagged `floor_fill`, and titled only while the day is short of two. A Chase against a known but
   unrelated film within 10%, or on a pace of up to 60 days, is emitted as a weak fallback (0.3). If fewer than two
   cards still clear the title fact check, the Slack header says so.
5. The card's reason names why a comparison film is notable ("a Best Picture winner and a 1992 classic") instead of the
   generic rule text.

Reference year is 2024 ($11.31, The Numbers). Update `data/ticket_prices.json` when a full-year 2025 average is verified.

## 6. Modern vs. Classic must be a real connection (added later on 2026-11-03)

New `src/faramir/links.py`. For every Chase/Overtake against a classic it records what connects the two films:
same franchise, same director, a shared top-billed actor, the same main genre, or two or more shared specific
TMDB keywords (generic ones like "sequel" or "murder" are ignored).

- **Label:** "Modern vs. Classic" now needs at least one link. A classic that is only "notable" (e.g. a Best Picture
  winner with no connection) stays a plain Chase/Overtake, still inflation-adjusted and stated as such.
- **MovieWeb demand:** the classic's director and top five cast are looked up in the Cerebro snapshot (News team,
  same rule as the rest of Faramir: 3+ articles, lift of 1.25x or more). Names that qualify are written on the card
  ("Brad Pitt (3.6x MovieWeb News baseline, 11 articles)") and passed to the titler as a CONNECTION line.
- **Ranking:** linked stories +0.1 strength, +0.05/+0.1 more for a medium/high-demand name. Floor fillers are untouched.
- **Fails safe:** no Cerebro snapshot = no demand line; TMDB keyword lookup failing = no theme link.
- Keywords are fetched only for the few classic candidates each day (two TMDB calls per candidate, cached).
